# The cyborg apparatus is the site of theory production
*Status: current | Date: 2026-04-28*

The apparatus includes the researcher, the AI, the codebook, the framework library, prior session traces, and the dialogue itself. Theory emerges through the apparatus, so reflexivity extends to the apparatus — surfacing AI gravity, codebook drift, framework choices — not just researcher positionality. The tool's job is to keep the situation legible to the researcher, so it stays steerable.

Extends Clarke's situational analysis: the AI is one of the actants, not outside the map.

**Important distinction**: the cyborg apparatus is the *site* of theory production, not the *object* of analysis. The researcher's data is still the object. Navel-gazing would be making the apparatus the object; keeping the apparatus visible is what makes it steerable in service of theorizing the data. Recursive self-implication (CGT-on-CGT-design) is a special case where the data happens to be the design sessions — not the general rule.
