# AI gravity is steerable, not hidden
*Status: current | Date: 2026-04-28*

The AI brings training-data gravity — defaults toward consolidation, similarity, plausible patterns, dominant framings. That gravity is part of the apparatus and therefore part of what reflexivity attends to (memo 003). Operationally: the tool surfaces the AI's default reading on demand ("what's the gravitational pull here?") and lets the researcher redirect — invoke a different lens (Reframe library), prime with sensitizing concepts, ask for the reading the AI is *not* defaulting to. Steerable, not hidden, not eliminated.

See memo 010 for the specific gravities CGT routes against, and memo 009 for the lens-not-application stance that governs how lenses (including the AI's gravity) operate in the apparatus.
