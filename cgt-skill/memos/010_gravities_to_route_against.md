# Gravities to route against, and the affordances we build instead
*Status: current | Date: 2026-04-28*

The AI's training pulls in specific directions that conflict with constructivist GT practice. Each pull has a counter-affordance the tool builds in. The point isn't to suppress the AI's defaults — it's to make the cyborg practice support specific moves the defaults won't reach on their own.

**Theming/categorizing → gerund coding and in-vivo language.**
What we want: codes that preserve action and process ("navigating trust," not "trust"), and codes that surface the participant/subject's own language as analytical material. Affordance: coding prompts hard-constrained to gerund forms; a distinct in-vivo surfacing pass that specifically identifies preserved language; "what's happening here?" as the prompt framing rather than "what is this about?"

**Early abstraction/explanation → staying close to data, postponing closure.**
What we want: initial coding that stays in the texture of the data without leaping to mechanisms or causes. Affordance: abstraction-tier markers in prompts (initial vs. focused vs. theoretical), with low-tier prompts explicitly resisting "underlying" claims; refusal to generate explanatory framings at initial coding; closure-postponement as a default the researcher can override.

**Coherence/resolution → holding tensions open.**
What we want: divergence preserved as data, not smoothed (memo 007). Affordance: divergence views that surface conflicting readings side-by-side; memo templates that include "tensions/unresolved" sections; refusal of "best reading" or "most likely" framings; the tool reports "these are in tension" rather than picking.

**Scientism register → constructivist language throughout.**
What we want: every prompt and every output uses constructivist framing. "I'm reading this as," "these codes emerged from my reading," "the AI suggests" — not "the data shows," "discovered," "reveals." Affordance: a register lint applied to tool outputs (voice-check infrastructure already exists in this repo and can be reused); prompt templates audited for register; flagged contamination handled silently the way voice-check does for June's writing.

**Dominant theoretical frameworks → surfaced sensitizing concepts and multi-tradition access.**
What we want: the researcher's tradition (including critical, minor, non-Western, indigenous frameworks) is foregrounded; the AI's default toward most-cited frameworks doesn't quietly shape coding. Crucially, **frameworks are lenses for reading, not categories the AI codes data into** (memo 009). Codes emerge from the data through the lens — they are not imposed. Affordance: Reframe library as a queryable resource for lens invocation; PROJECT.md captures researcher's tradition and sensitizing concepts at orientation; lens invocation is a first-class move during coding ("read this through X") with explicit guardrails against treating the lens as a coding scheme.

**Similarity pattern-matching → dialogic relation.**
What we want: constant comparison done dialogically — not "these records share a theme" but "this record takes up what that one opened." Affordance: prompt structures that specifically ask "what does this record ask or leave open?" and "where does another record take up this opening?" (other agent has offered to draft scaffolding here — this is where LLM gravity is hardest to fight). Generation tests for cross-record findings: does the finding live in any single record, or only in the relation? If only in relation, the relation IS the finding.

**Authoritative assertion → interpretive humility and visible uncertainty.**
What we want: AI outputs frame readings as readings, not as facts. Uncertainty visible. Alternative readings always available. Affordance: confidence/uncertainty marking on AI suggestions; "I'm reading this as" framing required; an "alternative readings" surface that always shows the reading the AI is not defaulting to.

**Coherent narrative → contradictions and multiple voices preserved.**
What we want: where data carries internal contradiction or multiple voices, those stay visible — not harmonized into a smooth account. Affordance: contradiction-tracking within and across records; multiple-voice tagging in memos; the tool refuses to write summaries that paper over conflicting data; layer-marking on extracts so source-voice is always visible.
