# AI as collaborative research partner
*Status: current | Date: 2026-04-28*

AI brings capacities and limits, as humans do. The cyborg practice is built on the bet that researcher and AI together reach what neither reaches alone. Design follows from that — but *where* dialogue is load-bearing vs. where AI working independently is fine is an empirical question, not a stance. Build a learning loop that surfaces it from use: track where the human's intervention changed the analysis vs. where it didn't, and let the tool's defaults evolve. Don't waste researcher attention on dialogue that isn't doing work.

That learning loop is itself the recursive commitment in operational form (the tool figures out its own dialogue requirements through use).
