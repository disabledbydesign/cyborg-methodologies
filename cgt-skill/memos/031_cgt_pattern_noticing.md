# CGT's pattern-noticing is metabolized differently, not outsourced
*Status: current | Date: 2026-04-28*

Clarification needed because the architectural commitment in memo 030 (external analytical tools as layer-marked supplementary surfaces) could read as "CGT only does openings; pattern-noticing happens elsewhere." That reading is wrong, and would itself be a binary collapse (memo 001).

## CGT does pattern-noticing in its own work

Constant comparison surfaces recurrence. Codes may recur across data. Categories develop partly by attending to what shows up repeatedly. Charmaz's "the people I interviewed sure do bring up X a lot" is a real CGT observation, not a TA artifact.

The methodological difference is what CGT *does* with the pattern, not whether CGT notices it.

## CGT metabolizes patterns differently from TA

CGT pattern-work asks:
- What's happening when this recurs? (Process, not theme)
- What varies across instances? (Variation as central, not noise to abstract away)
- What's the in vivo language doing in each instance? (Preservation, not abstraction)
- What does the pattern open? What questions does it raise that the data doesn't close? (Memo 016, openings)
- Who's doing the noticing? Whose register names this as a pattern? (Reflexivity, memo 023)
- What does the recurrence say about the conditions producing it? (Force relations, memo 015)

CGT pattern-work refuses:
- "What's the theme?" (consolidation)
- "What's the central concept here?" (early abstraction)
- Abstracting toward labels that erase variation (compression bias, memo 014)
- Treating the pattern as a finding-to-be-named rather than an observation-to-be-explored

## Concrete example

Multiple interview participants say "I felt overwhelmed."

**TA-style move**: this is the theme "Overwhelm." Cluster cases under it. Report it as a finding.

**CGT-style move**: what does each speaker mean by overwhelmed in their context (in vivo specificity)? When in their speech does the word appear (sequence, register)? What action or process is happening around the word (gerund-shaped reading)? What's *different* across instances of "overwhelmed" (variation)? What does the recurrence of this word in this corpus *open* about the conditions producing it (force relations, openings)?

Same observation. Different metabolization. CGT can fully notice the recurrence without it becoming a theme.

## Architectural point

External TA tools (memo 030) supply *additional* pattern surfaces when the researcher invokes them, layer-marked as supplementary. But CGT's own pattern-noticing is core practice, not a function delegated elsewhere. The architectural distinction is about **provenance of outputs** (whose methodology produced this artifact), not about which observations CGT can make.

Refuse the rigid version: "CGT only does openings; TA does patterns." That's the binary collapse memo 001 warns against. Patterns and openings are not different methodological territories; they're moves CGT makes in its own register, sometimes alongside external tools whose outputs flow in marked.
