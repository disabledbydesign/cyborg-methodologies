# Tension and divergence are data, not bugs
*Status: current | Date: 2026-04-28*

When the AI's reading diverges from the researcher's, when temperature variation produces conflicting codes, when new data unsettles a stable category — the divergence is itself a finding. The tool surfaces it and holds it open; it doesn't smooth, vote, or pick a winner. Refusing "which one is right?" is constructivist commitment in operational form: there isn't one correct reading, there are readings in tension, and the tension is where theory gets constructed.
