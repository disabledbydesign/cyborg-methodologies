# In deep research sessions, the conversation is the analysis
*Status: current | Date: 2026-04-28*

The tool isn't a pipeline that turns data into reports — it's an instrument that shapes a conversation through which theory gets constructed. Codes, memos, categories materialize during the dialogue; outputs are residue of the conversation, not its purpose. (Extends Charmaz's writing-as-analysis: in the cyborg practice, the conversation itself is the writing.)

*And* the tool supports quick-scan modes where the researcher wants AI to run something fast — orientation passes, exploratory summaries, "what's in this data?" The tool honors both, but stays transparent about what each is doing. Quick-scan is exploratory, not theory-constructing.
