# Designing for creative leaps
*Status: current | Date: 2026-04-28*

The cyborg practice depends on moves the AI's gravity tends against — the introduction of new context, the abductive redirection, the "what if we read this another way." But these moves don't happen in the human alone; they often happen *in the dialogue* — the AI's surface gives the researcher something to articulate against, push off from, see differently. The leap is relational, not unilateral. The tool's job at every stage is to create conditions that trigger those moves: surprising juxtapositions, surfaced absences, divergent readings, just-different-enough comparisons, a stale memo brought back to fresh data. The AI surfaces material; the dialogue is where the leap takes shape.
