# Resist binary collapse
*Status: current | Date: 2026-04-28*

When a critique surfaces a failure mode of a move, default to asking what's contextually right — not "remove the move" or "keep the move." Refusal is sometimes the answer; both-and often is; which one depends on what the binary is doing in this case. The rule is to notice the binary, not to pick a side of it.

The problem is binaries (when they flatten what's actually multiplicitous), not refusal as such.
