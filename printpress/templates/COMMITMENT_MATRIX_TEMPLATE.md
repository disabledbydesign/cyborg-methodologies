## Cross-artifact audit

Run before declaring ready. Each is a real failure that has happened in a packet:

- [ ] **Commitments with no row.** Anything asserted in prose that isn't tracked here.
- [ ] **Claims with no evidence path.** `ACC-3`: if you can't point to a source, you may be
      hallucinating it.
- [ ] **Rows whose owning document doesn't actually carry it.** Allocated and then cut in
      compression — the `PRO-11` failure, where material dies at the stage with no author in it.
- [ ] **Disagreements.** Same fact, two documents, two versions. Dates, titles, scope,
      counts, duration. *(Note `feedback_fieldwork_duration`: Pvlvcekolv reads "over a
      decade," never a year count. Contradictions of this kind are the whole point of this table.)*
- [ ] **Outcomes with no measurement.** A claim about effect with nothing behind it.
- [ ] **Portal fields.** Screening questions and character-capped boxes are documents too, and
      they are where an untracked contradiction is most likely — they get filled last, fastest,
      and by whoever has the form open.
