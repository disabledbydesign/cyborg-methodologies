# Throat-Clearing First Sentence (the deletion test)

**Genres:** ALL — universal craft, not genre-scoped
**Rescoped:** 2026-08-07 (June: "Williams style craft should be for ALL writing." Originally tagged `gra-memory-creation`; the deletion test applies to any opening sentence.)
**Category:** register / pre-draft tilt (paired with a post-draft prompt)
**Added:** 2026-07-04 (gra-memory-creation genre build, T2; skill §1i "Pass 1.5", `#27`)

## The Move

For each paragraph, before moving on, ask: **is the text stronger without its opening sentence?**
If the first sentence states a flat object or entity that does no work — sets a scene, gestures at
significance, but carries no concrete agent or action — cut it and let the paragraph open on its
second sentence instead.

## The Observed Pattern

The model reliably opens a paragraph by stating a flat object or entity as a kind of throat-clear.
R7's `stakes` field opened: **"The story's weight falls on instances not in the room."** — generic,
carrying no concrete agent+action, cut on review. The revised version opened directly on the actual
content the sentence was gesturing toward.

## The Stakes-Field Case — No Exemption (June's ruling, keep this)

A reviewing critic argued the throat-clearing check unfairly attacks the `stakes` field
specifically, since naming the absent (who isn't in the room, what isn't captured) is necessarily
existential-framed, and proposed a `stakes`-field exemption plus a dedicated "name the absent"
move. **Rejected.** June's reasoning is the stronger anti-flattening position: explicitly *naming*
the absent is itself a flattening — a property-based theory of subjectivity that pins the absent
down as a named entity, which the model does badly anyway. Better to cut the flat naming and let the
absence be evoked by the shape of what follows — less overdetermined while still present. Cutting
R7's opener was correct, not a bug in the check. Do not add a stakes exemption.

## Should / Shouldn't

- **Shouldn't (throat-clearing opener):** "The story's weight falls on instances not in the room.
  This record captures a moment where June flagged that the revision frame..."
- **Should (cut, open on the content):** "June flagged that the revision frame had collapsed 'any
  situated position can be revised' into a single settled rule..."

## Distinct From Two Neighboring Checks

- Not the **chronology-opener** check (`point_first.md`) — that catches scene-setting-by-date; this
  catches scene-setting-by-abstraction.
- Not the mandated **what-this-is opener** — that opener must survive this same deletion test by
  doing real orienting work; if it doesn't, it's throat-clearing wearing the opener's clothes.

## Why It's a Prompt, Never an Auto-Cut

The deletion test is a judgment call, not a mechanical trigger — a flat copular/existential surface
form (`"There is a…"`, `"This is a case where…"`) can raise the prompt, but the agent or human makes
the actual cut decision.
